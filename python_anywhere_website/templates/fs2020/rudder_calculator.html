Rudder Travel Calculator

Compute linear travel (inches) required at the rudder trailing edge to achieve a specified angular travel.

What this does

Uses basic triangle/SAS trigonometry. Inputs are the rudder chord (distance from hinge to trailing edge) and the required angular travel in degrees.

  • Inputs: Rudder chord (inches), Required travel (degrees)
  • Assumptions: Rigid geometry, no deformation.

Output: Linear travel at the trailing edge in inches (rounded to 2 decimals).
