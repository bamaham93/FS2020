O2 Bottle Pressure Calculator

Ported from the original gist. Computes expected pressure after a temperature change.

What this does

This calculator estimates the expected tank pressure after a temperature change using the relation P2 = (T2_K / T1_K) × P1.

  • Inputs: Initial temperature (°F), Final temperature (°F), Initial pressure (PSI).
  • Units: Temperatures must be in degrees Fahrenheit; pressures in PSI.
  • Assumptions: Ideal-gas-like proportional scaling with absolute temperature; no volume change or leaks.

Example: If a bottle is charged to 2000 PSI at 70 °F then warmed to 100 °F, expected pressure ≈ 2113.28 PSI.

Safety note: This tool is for estimation only. Do not rely on it for life-support or flight-critical decisions. Always follow equipment manuals and manufacturer guidance.
