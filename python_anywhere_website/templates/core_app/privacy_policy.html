Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 17, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Jacob McGowin ("we," "us," or "our") collects, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our website and messaging features.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information needed to provide our services, including:

  • Name, email address, and username (if you create an account)
  • Phone number (only if you choose to provide it)
  • SMS consent status and timestamp
  • Messages or form content you submit to us
  • Basic technical data such as IP address and browser type

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide website and messaging features
  • Send SMS messages only when you have opted in
  • Support your account and respond to requests
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse
  • Comply with legal requirements

4. SMS Messaging Terms

By providing your phone number, you agree to receive text messages from Jacob McGowin. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out, and reply HELP for support.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your phone number, SMS consent, or messaging data with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

We only share information when necessary to operate the service (for example, with our SMS delivery provider Twilio), or when legally required.

6. Data Retention and Security

We keep personal data only as long as needed to provide services and satisfy legal obligations. We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect data from unauthorized access.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us through the website contact form on the Resume page.

Twilio/A2P Compliance Summary

  • SMS opt-in and message data are not shared for marketing purposes
  • Users can opt out anytime by replying STOP
  • Users can get help anytime by replying HELP
